## Motivation and changes

The r07 numerical revision `81e6eae` failed in rounded hour 3 after 1,164.775
end-to-end seconds, with original-model residual 0.0831674221741. Its continuous
candidate was primal-feasible, but native dual infeasibility was poor. Finite
same-hour multipliers were transferred despite that poor quality. This is a
plausible initialization problem, not a controlled causal proof or evidence
that the source problem is infeasible. More than 6,000 global seconds remained.

Policy `network_slp_quality_guarded_recovery_v6` adds:

1. A same-hour multiplier-quality heuristic on the original model. Using the
   exact source objective gradient, original affine/nonlinear Jacobian and
   complete MOI multiplier map, compute the minimization-Lagrangian stationarity
   infinity norm divided by `max(1, norm(objective_gradient, Inf))`. Transfer
   duals only when this finite relative residual is at most 1e-3 and all older
   primal/mapping requirements pass. This is **not** a KKT certificate, a dual
   feasibility test or a global bound. Rejected multipliers do not discard the
   independently screened same-attempt primal.
2. When the continuous primal passes the existing 1e-10 internal target, use
   it directly for rounded repair even if its multipliers are rejected. Preserve
   its small primal interior-push settings; do not rerun an unsuccessful rounded
   linear-correction stage merely to replace usable primal initialization.
3. Query cheap original, unscaled native residuals at every eligible callback
   iteration instead of every fifth. The unchanged objective-window condition
   and complete mapped original-model audit still authorize any early stop.
4. After failed rounded repair, allow one adaptive-barrier primal-only recovery.
   It clears unsuitable duals, retains the same-attempt primal, uses at most the
   existing 120-second fallback cap, and protects the original absolute global
   deadline, future-hour reserves and existing 600-second maximum hour cap.
   It does not reset the clock. Failed recovery remains failure. Setting numerical
   recovery off explicitly disables this path.

Source equations, bounds, PMIN/PMAX, discrete domains, costs, penalties, temporal
